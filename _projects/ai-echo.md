Echocardiography is central to diagnosing and monitoring congenital heart disease, but expert interpretation and quantitative measurement are time-consuming and unevenly available. Our AI-Echo work develops deep learning methods that help clinicians identify congenital heart disease and automate clinically meaningful measurements from echocardiograms.
